  • At the top of a list the button is a wide "+ Add" pill sitting just above the bar, on the right.
  • Scrolling down squeezes it into a round + while you read. Scrolling down quickly tucks it into the bar so it's out of the way, and scrolling up brings it straight back.
  • Tapping it turns the + into ✕, dims the screen, and fans out Expense / Income / Transfer with a little bounce. Each one opens the keypad screen already set to that type.
  • All of this runs at 60 fps with spring motion, and the phone gives a light haptic tick when it opens.
