What maps to what

  • Tabs (Expenses / Income / Tags / Accounts) become simple text tabs with a mint underline.
  • Period: Week / Month / Year, plus the month button that opens the From/To wheels.
  • Big total with the comparison as a small line ("8% less than October").
  • Trend uses the same wave as Home. The previous period is a faint dashed line behind it (the existing "compare" option). Tap a point to see the value, or open that day's transactions.
  • The donut becomes a colored split bar like dd_analytics, above the category rows. Each row has a % badge in its category color.
  • Drill-down: tapping a category opens its subcategories in place. A small trail shows where you are ("All › Food & Dining"); tap ✕ or "All" to go back.
  • The ☰ at the top right holds the extra controls (account filter, compare on/off).
