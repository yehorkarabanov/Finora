Categories

  • Drill down instead of opening rows in place. With 3 levels, opening rows inside rows gets messy. Each tap replaces the list and the colored bar re-forms around the new level.
  • A row with subcategories shows "3 subcategories ›". A row without any shows its transaction count and opens those transactions.
  • "Directly in Food & Dining" (dashed icon) holds money assigned to the parent itself, since the app allows that. It always makes the numbers add up.
  • The trail at the top is All › Food › Restaurants. Tap any step to jump back; the phone's back gesture works too.

Tags

  • Nothing needs picking first. The tab opens on a ranking of your tags for the period, just like categories.
  • Tap one to open the detail you saw before. Long-press to pick several and combine them (Any / All).
  • Coming back to the tab reopens your last tag view. Leaving it with ✕ returns to the ranking.
